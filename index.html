Lens Guide

Which lens do you need?

A quick guide — the right lens depends on your prescription. Ask in store and we will explain what suits your eyes.

Single Vision

One power across the whole lens — for distance or for reading, whichever your prescription needs.

Most common

Bifocal

Two powers in one lens — distance on top, reading below. A classic, budget-friendly option.

Distance + reading

Progressive

Smooth change from distance to reading with no visible line — for people who need both, all day.

No visible line

Blue-Cut & Photochromic

Blue-cut eases screen strain; photochromic lenses darken in sunlight and clear indoors.

Screen & sun
